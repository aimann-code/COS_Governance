## Processing flow

1. Select component/entity and permitted create/update operation
2. Download or choose a versioned blank template/profile
3. Receive authorized source and register file/message/Sheet provenance
4. Parse into protected staging; preserve sheet/tab/row/cell/page/message positions
5. Suggest header mappings; confirm ambiguity; validate required fields and complete key
6. Preview matches/errors and execution policy; confirm preferences only if saving
7. Revalidate access/config/key and commit permitted domain writes transactionally
8. Record outcomes/history; reports read authorized virtual marts
